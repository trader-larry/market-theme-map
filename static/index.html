How to read this

Period sets the lookback. Lookbacks use trading sessions, so 1W is five sessions, 1M is 21, 3M is 63, 6M is 126 and 1Y is 252.

View switches between Themes (trades such as AI Semiconductors or GLP-1) and Sectors (the 11 standard GICS sectors, about nine large caps each). Compare the two to see whether a theme is leading its whole sector. A stock can be in both.

Rank by Move orders themes by average move, strongest first. Breadth orders by the share of names advancing. Name sorts alphabetically.

Theme figures are equal-weighted averages of their member names. The median is shown beside the average so one outsized mover doesn't distort the picture. Breadth counts advancing names.

Fair value is the analysts' mean price target from Yahoo Finance. Edit themes.json or sectors.json to change the groups and tickers.
